# Voting tool: Victorian state election 2026

A mobile-first web app for ordering your preferences for **Bendigo West** (lower house) and the **Northern Victoria Region** (upper house, above or below the line), then copying the numbers onto the real ballot paper.

- Reorder candidates with ▲▼; tap a name for a party summary and policy positions
- Below the line follows your above-the-line order, then can be fine-tuned
- "Show my ballot paper" lays your numbers out in ballot-paper order
- Save your order on your device, share it as a link, or save it as an image

Live at **https://vote.deverystreet.com**

Single self-contained `index.html`, no build step. Designed and built by Devery Street.
